Replay clips

Short MCAP recordings of ROS 2 bags behind the project showcase. Download one to open it on your own machine. The whole-body and clock-sync clips, with their metadata.yaml and layout files, are byte-identical copies of the showcase repository’s (main). The field EKF and localizer clips cover the same 20 s of a recorded match and carry the field and tag scene topics and a derived /viz/fix_points display topic; they and the point-cloud clip are hosted here and on the viewer’s R2 mirror.

The viewer buttons open the clip with its panel layout in Lichtblick, an open-source build of Foxglove Studio; no sign-in.

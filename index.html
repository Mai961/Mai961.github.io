Projects

Each project page starts with a clip and links to its module in my robotics showcase. The robot code itself is not open source: the showcase holds short, annotated excerpts of my own work, simplified to show how each piece works.

  • Shoot on the move

    A turret that keeps aiming while the robot drives, with shot compensation for the robot’s own velocity.

  • Arm placing objects

    Whole-body inverse kinematics on the coprocessor, with joint targets executed by the robot controller.

  • Arm tracking an AprilTag

    AprilTag fixes give the field pose that places the arm’s field-fixed goal.

  • Auto-tuning routine

    A PID tuning bench for an arm joint, steered by a coding agent inside hard limits.

  • Vision replay: a recorded match

    Tag detections and the estimated field pose, replayed from a recorded match.

  • Vision replay: the camera’s image stream

    The camera’s recorded image stream, replayed through the AprilTag localizer.
